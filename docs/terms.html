1. What p2pchat is

p2pchat is a peer-to-peer messenger. Messages are sent directly between users' devices and are end-to-end encrypted. We do not operate a central server that stores messages or group history. The signaling and TURN infrastructure only helps devices connect.

2. Prohibited content and behavior

  • Harassment, threats, hate speech, or sharing other people's private information without consent.
  • Spam, mass unsolicited messages, or attempts to disrupt the service.
  • Infringing intellectual property or other rights.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for the content you send and for the groups you create or join. Group owners manage their groups, including removing members. Because messages are end-to-end encrypted and stored on users' devices, we cannot read, delete or moderate message content on your or other users' devices.

Group ownership is managed by the participants. If the owner is inactive, or members start a challenge, the group may elect a new owner by an in-app vote. We do not mediate these decisions: the new owner receives the same group-management rights, and the previous owner may lose them. Group membership is frozen while an election is in progress.

4. Reporting and blocking

You can block a user locally and report a user or message in the app. Reports include metadata (such as public keys, group/message IDs, reason and optional screenshot) and are reviewed manually. We may, at our discretion, block a reported public key from our signaling/TURN infrastructure and cooperate with lawful requests. Blocking is local: other participants may still see the blocked user unless a group owner removes them.
