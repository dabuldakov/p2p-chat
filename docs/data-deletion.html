Data deletion

Last updated: October 23, 2026

p2pchat does not create accounts and does not store your messages or group history on our servers. Your data is stored on your device.

Delete data on your device

  • Delete individual messages in the chat (long-press → Delete).
  • Leave a group to remove it from the app.
  • Clear all app data: Android Settings → Apps → p2pchat → Storage → Clear data.
  • Uninstall the app — this removes its local data (including the identity key).

Messages already delivered to other participants remain on their devices; we cannot delete them remotely. You can ask the participants or the group owner to remove them.

Delete reports

If you submitted a report, you can request its deletion by emailing dabuldakov@gmail.com. Include the approximate date/time and, if possible, your public key (short ID). We delete reports on request within 30 days; reports are also deleted automatically within 90 days.

There is no account to delete. Your identity is a key stored only on your device; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. Keep your seed phrase if you want to restore the identity later.
